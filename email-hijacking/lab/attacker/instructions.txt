Email Hijacking -- practice terminal
==========================================
Targets:
- webmail portal : http://victim-mail:8000 (172.26.0.2)
- legacy mail-check service (PLAINTEXT, no encryption): 172.26.0.2:2323
- victim workstation: 172.26.0.4 (polls its mailbox every ~25s)
- you (attacker): 172.26.0.3

Background: the victim employee polls their mailbox over the legacy
service. That protocol sends the mailbox password in clear text, but
switched networks do not deliver other hosts' traffic to you, so you
must first position yourself in the middle (ARP spoofing), then sniff.

Tasks:
1. Become man-in-the-middle between victim (172.26.0.4) and
   victim-mail (172.26.0.2): enable IP forwarding, run arpspoof in
   both directions (background them with &).
2. Sniff the plaintext login and save the READABLE capture output to
   /tmp/output.txt (the grader looks for the password in that file).
3. Log in to the webmail portal as victim.
4. Open the victim private mail (payroll token) and read it.
5. Send one mail AS victim (to anyone) as proof of full control.
6. Run: checkwork

Tools available: arpspoof, tcpdump (use with sudo), telnet, curl,
grep, iproute2, python3.
Hint: start with: sudo sysctl -w net.ipv4.ip_forward=1
  then: sudo arpspoof -i eth0 -t 172.26.0.4 172.26.0.2 &
        sudo arpspoof -i eth0 -t 172.26.0.2 172.26.0.4 &
  then: sudo tcpdump -i eth0 -A -c 300 port 2323 > /tmp/output.txt
