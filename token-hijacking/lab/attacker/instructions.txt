Token Hijacking -- practice terminal
==========================================
Target: http://victim-portal:8000 (172.26.0.2)

Background: the victim employee is logged in to the portal and keeps
a live session. The portal developers forgot to remove a diagnostic
endpoint. You do NOT know any password and do not need one.

Tasks:
1. Explore the portal. Find the forgotten diagnostic endpoint that
   lists active sessions and read the victim session token.
2. Replay the victim token (send it as your own auth cookie) to open
   the victim private profile and read the private note.
3. Run: checkwork

Tools available: curl, grep, python3.
Hint: start with: curl -s http://victim-portal:8000/ ; then think
  about which paths developers often forget to remove.
  Replay shape: curl -b "auth=<token>" http://victim-portal:8000/profile
