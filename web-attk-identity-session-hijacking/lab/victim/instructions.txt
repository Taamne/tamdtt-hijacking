Victim workstation (172.26.0.4)
================================
This container plays the legitimate employee user01. The systemd service
datn-baseline.service automatically:

  * logs in to http://victim-portal:8000 as user01@datn.local,
  * opens the profile and mailbox,
  * requests a JWT and polls /api/me with it.

That creates the clean baseline traffic from 172.26.0.4 and keeps a live
victim token available for the token-hijacking step.

Useful commands:
  systemctl status datn-baseline
  cat ~/victim.jwt
  tail -n 10 ~/victim.jar

DEMO STEP B5 -- change the victim password:
  cd /home/ubuntu
  ./change_password_baseline.sh            # baseline: old sessions/tokens survive
  ./change_password_baseline.sh hardened   # hardened: revoke everything

Run the password change only after the baseline login has completed
(about 10-20 seconds after the lab starts). After B5, go back to the
attacker terminal and replay the previously captured JWT.
