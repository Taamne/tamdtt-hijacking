Email Forwarding Detection -- SOC workstation
==================================================
Target mailbox lives on victim-mail (http://victim-mail:8000).
The victim mailbox has MANY old forwarding rules; ONE of them is a
rogue exfiltration rule planted by the attacker (threat emulator).

Workflow:
1. Pull the evidence (mail log + mailbox DB mirror):
     ./detector/fetch_logs.sh
2. Diff current rules against the approved inventory and spot the
   unapproved one(s):
     python3 detector/detect.py --diff
3. Triage the suspect: check WHEN it was created (mail.log) and
   whether its domain looks legitimate:
     grep -a FORWARD_RULE_SET ~/collected/mail.log | tail
     grep -a MAILBOX_LOGIN_SUCCESS ~/collected/mail.log | tail
4. Fix detector/rules.json (see: python3 detector/detect.py --help),
   then run detection:
     python3 detector/detect.py --detect
   Expected when fixed: exactly ONE alert on the rogue rule and
   SUMMARY tp=1 fp=0 (no alert on the approved partner/internal rules).
5. Run: checkwork

Notes:
- Starter rules only match known-bad IOCs (stale intel) and miss the
  lookalike domain on purpose: your job is the allowlist fix.
- /tmp/output.txt convention from the attack labs is NOT used here;
  graded artifacts are alerts.log (server-log derived) + rules.json.
